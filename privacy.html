Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2025  ·  Effective: April 14, 2025

PV Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Atlas.ai mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and password when you create an account.
  • Profile Information: Your display name, social media handle, content pillars, and platform preferences.
  • Photos and Media: With your explicit permission, we access your device's camera roll to analyze and cluster your content. Photos are processed locally on your device and via our AI services. We do not store your photos on our servers.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, feature usage, and performance data to improve our services.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and push notification tokens.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Atlas.ai service
  • To personalize your content recommendations based on your pillars
  • To send push notifications about your content (with your permission)
  • To analyze app usage and fix bugs
  • To communicate with you about your account

3. Photo and Camera Roll Access

Atlas.ai requests access to your photo library solely to analyze and cluster your content into pillars. This analysis is performed using AI vision services. We do not upload, store, or share your personal photos with third parties. You can revoke photo access at any time in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API: For AI-powered photo analysis and caption generation. Data processed is subject to Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Base44: For backend authentication and data storage.
  • Expo: For push notification delivery.
  • Meta (Instagram API): If you connect your Instagram account, we access your profile and media data as authorized by you.

5. Instagram and Meta Data

If you connect your Instagram account, we collect only the data necessary to display your analytics and enable post scheduling. We do not sell or share your Instagram data with third parties. You can disconnect your Instagram account at any time in Settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at support@pvlabs.co. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and keep your account credentials confidential.

8. Children's Privacy

Atlas.ai is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Data portability

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
